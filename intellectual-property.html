§ 02 Framework alignment

Strong alignment with major security frameworks.

Research program
Provisional filings
from August 2025

The portfolio addresses security problems now central to U.S. and allied cybersecurity frameworks.

Its focus — continuous authentication, dynamic authorization, least privilege, delegated authority, runtime verification, cryptographic accountability, revocation, and fail-secure control of autonomous systems — shows strong conceptual and architectural alignment with the Department of Defense Zero Trust architecture and NSA's 2026 implementation guidance, NIST's AI Agent Identity and Authorization initiative, the joint Five Eyes agentic-AI guidance, and subsequent NSA AI Security Center guidance.

The research program traces to provisional filings beginning August 19, 2025. Portions of the portfolio were filed before several of these 2026 publications — including the April 2026 Five Eyes guidance and the May 2026 NSA AI-security guidance, with the parent application predating the January 2026 NSA Zero Trust implementation guidelines.
