§ 01 Research program

Four lines of inquiry into trust under autonomy.

01
Autonomous-agent security
Delegated authority, runtime authorization, authorization-artifact lifecycle management, bounded autonomy, and enforcement for agents acting on behalf of human principals.
02
Continuous trust
Continuously evaluating identity assurance and adapting access as authentication confidence, device state, behavioral signals, and context change.
03
Multimodal security
Combining biometric, behavioral, visual, contextual, and cross-platform signals for identity assurance and deception detection under uncertainty.
04
Private communications
Communication systems built around end-to-end encryption, minimal infrastructure trust, and user-controlled security.
