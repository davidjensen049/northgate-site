Security research & engineering

Security architecture for a world of autonomous systems.

Northgate Cryptography is an independent security research and engineering company working on privacy-preserving communications, continuous trust, and authorization architectures for autonomous AI systems.

Research areas

Autonomous-agent security

Delegated authority, runtime authorization, authorization-artifact lifecycle management, bounded autonomy, and enforcement controls for AI agents acting on behalf of human principals.

Continuous trust

Architectures for continuously evaluating identity assurance and adapting access as authentication confidence, device state, behavioral signals, and operating context change.

Multimodal security

Combining biometric, behavioral, visual, contextual, and cross-platform signals for identity assurance, fraud detection, and trust assessment under uncertainty.

Private communications

Communication systems designed around end-to-end encryption, minimal infrastructure trust, and user-controlled security.

Independent research

A U.S. patent portfolio in continuous trust and agent authorization

Northgate Cryptography founder David Jensen has independently developed three related pending U.S. patent families spanning continuous identity assurance, multimodal biometric and deception analysis, and runtime authorization controls for autonomous AI agents.

The research program began in 2025 and has expanded into a broader investigation of how trust, identity, and authority should behave as humans increasingly delegate actions to autonomous systems.
