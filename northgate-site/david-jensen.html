Founder

David Jensen

Offensive-security leader · independent inventor · founder of Northgate Cryptography.

David Jensen is an offensive-security practitioner, independent inventor, and founder of Northgate Cryptography whose work spans adversary emulation, security architecture, continuous identity assurance, privacy-preserving communications, and authorization controls for autonomous AI systems.

Professionally, Jensen has built and led enterprise offensive-security capabilities within a large regulated healthcare environment — including full-scope penetration testing, assumed-breach exercises, adversary emulation, detection validation, exploitability analysis, and executive risk communication — architecting an internal detection-and-validation platform that maps executed techniques to MITRE ATT&CK coverage for repeatable assessment.

Independent research

Jensen has independently authored and prosecuted three related pending U.S. patent families:

Runtime authorization of autonomous AI agents

Continuous constraint of delegated agent authority through changing trust signals, permission tiers, authorization-artifact lifecycle controls, pre-execution enforcement, delegation-chain propagation, and fail-secure operation.

Continuous biometric authentication within zero-trust environments

Continuous reassessment of identity assurance during active sessions, including adaptive thresholds, dynamic privilege adjustment, presentation-attack detection, and risk-responsive authentication.

Multimodal biometric security and deception analysis

Machine-learning architectures combining visual, biometric, behavioral, contextual, and cross-platform signals to assess identity and deception risk under uncertainty.

He also researches how continuous-authentication systems should be evaluated statistically, and how autonomous systems change conventional assumptions around identity, delegation, and least privilege.
